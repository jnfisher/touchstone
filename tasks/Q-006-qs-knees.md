## Goal
Rows R11, R12 and R13 of `docs/quicksilver-profile.md` are live under `Profile::Quicksilver`: the crit
knee, the magic resistance stat knee and the dodge agility knee read the profile's `Balance`. Under
`Profile::Stock` and `Profile::Quicksilver(&Balance::NEUTRAL)` every existing fixture gives exactly
the answer it gives now; under `Balance::V120` the answers equal the engine vectors in
`fixtures/quicksilver/knees.json`.

## Read
- `docs/quicksilver-profile.md` parts 1 (R11 to R13), 4 and 5 (this is the Q-006 row).
- `docs/testing.md` (Quicksilver vectors and the identity harness), `tasks/Q-005-qs-vectors.md`.
- MME through the index: modMMudFunc `CalcMR`, `CalcDodge`; the crit block of `CalcAttack`.
- `crates/touchstone-rules/src/quicksilver/` (`Balance::knee_third`), and the three call chains:
  `calc_mr` (`char_panel::refresh_magic_res`, `gear/second.rs`), `calc_dodge` (`gear/second.rs`,
  `paste_party.rs`), the crit block in `attack.rs`.

## Do
1. `calc_mr` and `calc_dodge` take a `Profile` first (like `calc_dodge_vs_accuracy`). Under
   Quicksilver the stat part is compressed with `Balance::knee_third` before the stock arithmetic:
   `(Int + 3 Wil) / 4` for MR (gear and spells are added after), Agility for the `(Agi - 50) / 3`
   term of dodge. Stock is untouched. `refresh_magic_res` takes the profile too; the pipeline,
   `gear/second.rs` and `paste_party.rs` pass the profile they already hold.
2. The crit block of `attack_steps` moves into a small pub function `crit_after_knee(profile, crit)`
   (the knee, then MME's cap of 99). Stock keeps knee 40; Quicksilver reads `Balance::crit_knee`.
   `calculate_attack` threads the profile into `attack_steps`. The cap of 99 stays MME's: the
   vectors are the uncapped engine value, so the test compares against `min(v, 99)`.
3. Tests: `quicksilver_vectors.rs` asserts the `v120` column of every knee case through
   `Profile::quicksilver_v120()` and the `neutral` column through `Profile::Stock` and through
   `Quicksilver(&NEUTRAL)`. The identity harness also runs the pipeline, gear and paste-party
   fixtures under NEUTRAL where those tests can take a profile.
4. `docs/differences.md`: the knees act on the character's own stats only; a monster's magic
   resistance and dodge are data and are not compressed (R12, R13 say player only).
